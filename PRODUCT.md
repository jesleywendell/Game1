# Product

## Register

product

## Users
PC players, aged 16-30, who enjoy independent 2D action games and short-run roguelites (similar to Hades fans). They seek mechanical challenge, positioning-heavy combat, and a dark fantasy atmosphere.

## Product Purpose
UI and environmental design for "OATHBREAKER", a 2D isometric Action RPG. The interface must support fast-paced combat and roguelite progression without breaking the dark, corrupted immersion of the game world.

## Brand Personality
Dark Fantasy, somber, corrupted, melancholic, tense. 

## Anti-references
- Bright, bubbly mobile game aesthetics
- Overly complex, spreadsheet-like MMO interfaces
- Clean, corporate SaaS minimalism
- "Glassmorphism" or overly modern web UI trends that clash with pixel art

## Design Principles
- **Embrace the gloom:** Use shadows and deep, muted colors (dark blues, moss greens, tarnished golds, blood reds) to reinforce the cursed setting.
- **Clarity in combat:** Essential information (HP, cooldowns) must be instantly readable via shape and color, even in chaotic isometric battles.
- **Sacrifice over safety:** The UI and visual feedback should reflect the game's core theme of corrupted holy powers costing blood/HP.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- High contrast for critical combat UI elements against dark backgrounds.
- Clear visual indicators for interactive elements in the Hub.
